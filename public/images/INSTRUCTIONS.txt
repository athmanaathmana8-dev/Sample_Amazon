HOW TO ADD YOUR CAMERA PICTURE:

1. Place your camera/webcam image file in this folder (public/images/)
2. Name it exactly: webcam.jpg
   (Or if it's a different format: webcam.png, webcam.webp, etc.)
3. If you use a different filename, update products.js accordingly

Supported formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp, .gif

The image will automatically appear in the Webcam HD product card!
